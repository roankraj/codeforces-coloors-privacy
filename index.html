Privacy Policy

Codeforces Coloors — Dark Mode & More

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Overview

Codeforces Coloors is a Chrome extension that customizes the appearance of Codeforces with color themes and Focus Mode. This policy explains how the extension handles information.

Data Collection

The extension does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. It does not use analytics or tracking.

Local Storage

The extension uses Chrome's chrome.storage.local API to save your selected theme and Focus Mode preference. These settings are stored locally in your browser and are used to provide the extension's features.

The extension does not send these settings to the developer.

Third-Party Resources

The extension loads certain images from external servers, including Cloudinary and Codeforces. When your browser requests these images, the respective servers may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address.

The handling of information by those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Data Sharing and Use

The developer does not receive or sell your saved settings or browsing data. The extension does not use user data for advertising, profiling, or purposes unrelated to its features.

Data Security and Retention

Theme and Focus Mode preferences remain in Chrome's local extension storage until they are changed or the extension's stored data is cleared or removed.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
